Safety guidance

Your safety comes before a report.

In immediate danger?

Move to a safer place and contact local emergency services or appropriate security personnel. CrimeAlert is a reporting platform and is not an emergency dispatch service.

Before you report

  • Prioritise your immediate safety and avoid confronting anyone.
  • Only share details you can provide safely and accurately.
  • Do not include unnecessary personal information about yourself or others.

Using media responsibly

  • Only upload material you are allowed to share.
  • Avoid capturing or sharing content that could put people at further risk.
  • Use the anonymous option when it better protects your privacy.
