About CrimeAlert

Better reporting can lead to better-informed safety decisions.

CrimeAlert is a location reporting platform designed to help communities record incidents clearly, responsibly and with useful geographic context.

Our purpose

Make the first report easier to make.

Delayed or incomplete reporting makes it harder to understand safety challenges. The platform brings the essential details together: incident type, description, time, location and optional media.

Location-aware

Reports can include GPS or map-selected coordinates for better local context.

Privacy-minded

People can submit anonymously, and private reporter data is not displayed publicly.

What the platform supports

Communities

Structured reporting for urban neighbourhoods, estates and local groups.

Campuses & organisations

A clearer way to document incidents within shared spaces.

Safety teams

Review-ready information that can support monitoring and analysis.
